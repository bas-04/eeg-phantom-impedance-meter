======================================================================

31 pairs. Ranked by mean downhill gradient = height drop / arc length,
computed on a hemisphere model where the flattened 10-20 map radius is
the polar angle from the vertex (map r=1 at ear level = 90 degrees).

Two distinct risk mechanisms are represented, and they rank oppositely:

  RUNNING  gel flows downhill. Driven by slope, so the peripheral pairs
           dominate - the whole outer ring sits on a near-vertical wall.
  SMEARING gel spreads sideways. Driven by proximity, independent of
           slope. This is the Cz cluster, the flattest pairs in the set.

Priority tiers for the interleaved sweep (test high-risk pairs more often):
   tier 1  gradient >= 0.80   15 pairs   test every cycle
   tier 2  gradient 0.60-0.80  8 pairs   test every 2nd cycle
   tier 3  gradient <  0.60    8 pairs   test every 3rd cycle
   mean measurements per cycle: 21.7

CORRECTED 24 OCT 2026: Fp2 is DB25 pin 14 on both J1 and J2, checked against
the Rev B board file. Earlier copies of this table listed it as pin 12, which
is not connected to anything - a test resistor wired there shows nothing. Pins
12, 13 and 25 are unused on both connectors. The firmware was never affected;
its pair table uses site numbers, not pins.

GND EXCLUDED FROM CHECKING (5 Nov 2026): the three pairs involving GND
(Fz-GND, GND-Fp1, GND-Fp2) are still listed below but are NOT scanned by
default. On the phantom the GND and Fz inserts are 4 mm apart, so gel reaching
the GND insert is close to unavoidable, and the conductive insert completes a
bridge that on a real head would need gel to reach the other electrode's gel.
GND is still measured and must still be prepped. To check the GND pairs again,
set BRIDGE_CHECK_GND to true in phantom_meter_v2.ino.

BENCH TEST: one resistor, 470 ohm to 1 kohm, from the J1 pin to the J2
pin shown. J1 is the PHANTOM cable, J2 the ELECTRO-CAP cable. Direction
matters for a two-terminal resistor: wire J1 on the uphill site and J2 on
the downhill site, matching the firmware's table order. A real gel bridge
wets all four nodes and is detected either way; a lone resistor is not.

DIRECTION IS DIAGNOSTIC. Every pair has an uphill and a downhill site, so
the display can draw an arrow rather than a plain line and tell the student
which end to clean first - gel pools at the downhill site.

DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED (decided 14 Oct 2026, do not re-add without cause):
   Fp1-Fp2  and  O1-O2.  Both sites of each pair are at the same height, so
   gradient is zero and the risk is pure sideways smearing. The midline
   routes are already covered: GND-Fp1 and GND-Fp2 down the forehead,
   Pz-O1 and Pz-O2 down the occiput.

THRESHOLD: trip below ~50 kohm, clear above ~80 kohm. Hysteresis is
required or a marginal bridge chatters. Tune after measuring a real gel
bridge on the finished phantom; the value depends on contact geometry.

MODEL CAVEAT: hemisphere. A printed head is more ellipsoidal - flatter on
top, steeper on the sides - which sharpens the contrast rather than
softening it. A real head also has a face below Fp1/Fp2 rather than
continuing spherically. The ranking is robust to both; the absolute
gradient numbers are not.
